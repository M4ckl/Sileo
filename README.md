## About The Project

In a world full of digital noise and constant notifications, **Sileo** offers a premium environment to disconnect and recharge. Built with a core philosophy of minimalism and intentional design, it is not just a timer, but a tool for building healthy digital habits. 

Take a moment to pause, listen to immersive ambient soundscapes, and earn achievements for your consistency.

### Key Features
* **Interactive Focus Timer:** A beautifully crafted, gesture-based interface (bezel control) for setting your pause duration.
* **Ambient Audio Engine:** Seamless soundscapes (rain, forest, fire, meditation) designed for deep focus and relaxation.
* **Mindful Gamification:** Earn medals like "Early Bird" and "Zen Master" as you build your streak and track your progress over time.
* **Dynamic Aesthetics:** Switch between multiple premium color themes (Glassmorphism UI) on the fly to match your current mood.
